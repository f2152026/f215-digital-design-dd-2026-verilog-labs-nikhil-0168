(d) Observations

Where the delay is placed affects how the output behaves.
When inputs change quickly, the three implementations can give different results, and this difference becomes more noticeable as the delay value increases.

(e) What I learned

The position of a delay statement in Verilog is significant.
A delay placed before the assignment postpones when the inputs are read, whereas an intra-assignment delay reads the inputs right away but postpones when the output is updated.
